Methodology

Four inputs. One defensible number.

The model doesn't guess — it weighs measurable inputs the same way a market would, then documents exactly how it got there.

PERF

On-field / on-court performance

Position-adjusted stats benchmarked against your level of competition.

REACH

Social & audience reach

Followers, engagement rate, and content consistency across platforms.

COMP

Market comparables

Disclosed and estimated deal values for athletes in a similar tier and sport.

GEO

Regional brand demand

Local sponsor density and how much your market pays for visibility.

VALUE = w1·PERF + w2·REACH + w3·COMP + w4·GEO
w1–w4 = weights calibrated per sport & level
output = annual value range + confidence band
Every report shows its inputs and weights — nothing is a black box. You can challenge any number in it.
